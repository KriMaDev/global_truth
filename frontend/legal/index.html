Corrections and removal

If something here is wrong, if you are a publisher and want your headlines removed, or if a headline names you and you want it taken down, write through krimadev.com/contact. Requests are handled promptly.

Disclaimer

  • Everything is provided as is, for general information. It can be incomplete, late or wrong.
  • Do not use this site for safety decisions. Disaster alerts from GDACS are automated estimates and do not replace official warnings: follow your national and local authorities.
  • News locations are worked out by software at the GDELT Project from the article text and can be wrong; such events say so on their card.
  • Headlines and articles belong to their publishers and reflect their views, not ours.

Privacy

This site collects as little as possible: no account, no cookies, no ads, no tracking across websites.

  • Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and the page requested. Cloudflare keeps this data for a short time in logs, which we can look at to fix problems or stop attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the site securely (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). Cloudflare is a US company certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found to give adequate protection.
  • No cookies, and visits counted without knowing who you are. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It records page views, the site that sent you here, your country and your type of device, so we can see which parts of the site are useful. We see only totals, never who you are. Its small script loads from static.cloudflareinsights.com. The legal basis is the same legitimate interest as above.
  • Almost everything comes from this site. Fonts, map data and events are served from globe.krimadev.com itself. Links to articles open the outlet's own site, under its own privacy policy.
  • Sharp Earth images when you zoom in close. Only then, your browser loads map tiles directly from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov). Like any website, NASA's servers receive your IP address and browser details for those requests; no cookies are involved. See NASA's privacy policy. Without them, the globe still works with its own images, just less sharp close up.
  • Stored in your browser. Only whether you left the legend open on a phone, under the key wng-legend-open, and the text size you chose, under the key wng-text-size. They never leave your device; clear them in your browser settings at any time.
  • Your rights. You can ask what data about you is processed, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its use or object to it, and complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be). If you write to us through krimadev.com/contact, krimadev.com's privacy policy explains how your message is handled.
