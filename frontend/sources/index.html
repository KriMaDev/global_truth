News outlets

Only articles from these outlets appear. They were chosen to cover every region and a range of viewpoints.

  • State-affiliated means funded and editorially controlled by a government, without legal editorial independence. The label is shown on every item from that outlet.
  • Public means publicly funded, with editorial independence guaranteed by law or charter.
  • Private covers companies, trusts and non-profits.
  • Intergovernmental is an international organisation's news service.
  • Balance: to keep any one outlet from dominating, at most 15 news events from each are kept per day (the most severe, most widely reported and newest), and at most 5 sport events, counted apart.

What is left out

  • Culture, lifestyle, travel, food, shopping and opinion pages, podcasts, video and live pages, and paid "promoted" or "sponsored" pages.
  • Articles in none of the eleven categories, and news whose headline names no place.
  • Outlets' other-language editions: only the English edition is used for DW, France 24, RFI, Meduza, Anadolu Agency and NHK World. The Hindu's city pages are left out too.
  • GDACS's Green alerts for wildfires and droughts, of which there are dozens at any time.
  • Article text: no snippets are shown for news, and nothing beyond the headline is stored.
